Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our development process, application support, and standards.

What platforms do you build applications for? +
We specialize in cross-platform development with Flutter, delivering native applications for iOS (App Store), Android (Google Play Store), and modern responsive web browsers from a single, battle-tested codebase.
How do you handle user privacy and tracking compliance? +
We treat user privacy as a fundamental right. On iOS, we fully implement the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission system before accessing advertising identifiers. On Android, we support Advertising ID reset options. You can read our complete Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details.
Can Alisa Tech Studio build custom software for my company? +
Yes! In addition to our published consumer applications, we collaborate with startups and established enterprises to develop tailored software, API integrations, and mobile solutions. Reach out via our contact form below to discuss your project scope.
What is your typical software maintenance timeline? +
We provide continuous monitoring through telemetry tools such as Firebase Crashlytics. We routinely update our software whenever Apple or Google releases new major mobile OS versions to guarantee compatibility and security.
How can I report a bug or request a feature in one of your apps? +
You can email our support desk directly at alisatechstudio@gmail.com. We inspect all incoming user feedback and roll out fixes in our bi-weekly release cycles.
