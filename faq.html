Mobile & Software Engineering

What platforms do you build applications for? +
We specialize in cross-platform development with Flutter, delivering native applications for iOS (Apple App Store), Android (Google Play Store), and modern responsive web browsers from a single, battle-tested codebase. This guarantees feature parity and cuts maintenance overhead in half.
Do your Flutter apps achieve true 60 and 120 FPS performance? +
Yes, absolutely. We apply strict rendering optimizations: widget tree pruning, RepaintBoundary layer isolation, const constructors, and Dart isolate concurrency for CPU-heavy tasks like image decoding or JSON parsing. With Flutter’s Impeller engine on iOS and Vulkan on modern Android, our apps maintain fluid 60/120 FPS scrolling without micro-stutters.
Can you migrate an existing native Android or iOS app to Flutter? +
Yes. We regularly help organizations modernize legacy codebases by migrating Java/Kotlin and Objective-C/Swift code into unified Flutter apps. We preserve existing SQLite databases, keychain credentials, and user data models to ensure seamless upgrades without user data loss.
How do you manage offline capabilities and data synchronization? +
We architect our software with an offline-first philosophy. Local device storage is managed via SQLite or encrypted Hive boxes. When network connectivity is established, local changes are synchronized with cloud backends (Firebase Firestore or custom REST/GraphQL APIs) using robust conflict-resolution algorithms.

User Privacy & Regulatory Compliance

How do you handle user privacy and tracking compliance? +
We treat user privacy as a fundamental human right. On iOS, we strictly implement the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission system before reading advertising identifiers (IDFA). On Android, we support Google Advertising ID reset controls and anonymized telemetry. Review our complete Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for detailed disclosures.
Does Alisa Tech Studio collect or sell personal user information? +
No. We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal user data to third-party data brokers. Telemetry is collected in aggregated, pseudonymized formats purely to diagnose app crashes, monitor network latency, and improve software stability.
Are your applications compliant with GDPR and COPPA? +
Yes. For European users, our web applications implement Google Consent Mode v2 and transparent cookie governance banners. For mobile games and educational apps suitable for younger audiences, we enforce COPPA-compliant configurations and disable behavioral interest tracking.

Client Projects, Quotes & Delivery

Can Alisa Tech Studio build custom software for my company? +
Yes! In addition to our published consumer applications, we collaborate with startups and established enterprises to develop tailored software, API integrations, and mobile solutions. Reach out via our Contact Us page or email alisatechstudio@gmail.com to discuss your project scope.
What is the typical development timeline for a new mobile application? +
Timelines vary based on scope and architectural complexity. A focused MVP utility application typically launches within 4 to 8 weeks. Comprehensive platforms with real-time backends, social feeds, and payment gateways generally take 10 to 16 weeks from discovery to store approval.
What deliverables are provided at the conclusion of a project? +
You receive full intellectual property ownership, complete Git source code repositories, Figma UI/UX design assets, API documentation, CI/CD deployment pipelines, and compiled production release bundles for both iOS and Android.

App Support, Updates & Bug Reports

What is your typical software maintenance timeline? +
We provide continuous monitoring through telemetry tools such as Firebase Crashlytics. We routinely update our software whenever Apple or Google releases new major mobile OS versions to guarantee ongoing compatibility, security patches, and performance optimizations.
How can I report a bug or request a feature in one of your apps? +
You can email our developer support desk directly at alisatechstudio@gmail.com. Please include your device model, OS version, and any relevant screenshots or steps to reproduce. Our engineering team reviews incoming reports daily.
