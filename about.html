About Our Engineering Studio

Building thoughtful, performant, and privacy-conscious software solutions that empower millions of mobile and web users worldwide.

Who We Are

Alisa Tech Studio is an independent software engineering and digital product development studio founded with a singular focus: crafting high-speed, reliable, and beautifully designed digital tools. Headquartered in Naria, Shariatpur, Bangladesh, our team combines technical craftsmanship with modern software architecture to develop cross-platform mobile apps, utility tools, educational platforms, and digital games.

Since our inception, we have published multiple top-tier applications on Google Play, ranging from cognitive brain training puzzles like Sudoku Classic and Math Puzzles to comprehensive language learning suites such as Learn German and Learn Italian, along with essential everyday productivity tools.

Our Guiding Mission: To bridge the gap between technical complexity and everyday utility by shipping software products that launch fast, respect user privacy, and operate reliably across every screen size and mobile device.

Our Engineering Philosophy

Modern digital experiences are frequently bloated with sluggish rendering engines and invasive data collection. At Alisa Tech Studio, we adhere strictly to counter-principles:

⚡ 60fps & 120fps Smooth Performance

We leverage Google's Flutter framework with Dart to deliver silky smooth transitions, minimal cold-start times, and zero UI stuttering on devices from budget Android phones to flagship iPhones.

🔒 Privacy-First Design

We believe user privacy is an inviolable right. Our applications request minimal runtime permissions and adhere strictly to Google Play User Data policies, Apple ATT, and GDPR/CCPA standards.

🧩 Clean Architecture

Every software project is architected with clear separation of concerns, robust state management (Bloc and Riverpod), and decoupled presentation and data layers for effortless maintainability.

🌍 Global Accessibility

Our applications feature internationalized strings, responsive tablet and phone layouts, high-contrast accessible typography, and intuitive user interfaces suitable for learners worldwide.

Our Product Portfolio

Our development catalog encompasses four primary categories of digital software:

  • Educational Applications: Structured, syllabus-based language learning applications for German and Italian, combining CEFR level progression, interactive grammar exercises, and vocabulary retention algorithms.
  • Brain Training & Logic Games: Classic puzzle games like Sudoku and Math Puzzles engineered with custom puzzle generation algorithms, offline saves, and zero disruptive interruptions.
  • Everyday Utilities: High-performance QR & barcode scanners, personal budget organizers, and shopping list assistants engineered to optimize everyday workflows without unnecessary complexity.
  • Connected Digital Experiences: Real-time fixture telemetry trackers and modern communication applications built on resilient cloud architectures.

Quality Assurance & Ongoing Maintenance

Publishing an application is merely the starting line. We actively monitor software telemetry using tools such as Firebase Crashlytics to catch and resolve edge-case exceptions before they impact user workflows. Whenever Google or Apple releases new mobile operating system updates (such as Android 15 or iOS 18), we proactively update target SDK levels, audit permission workflows, and optimize background processing routines.
